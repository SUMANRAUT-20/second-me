# Second Me 🌙

**You sleep. Your Second Me works.**

An AI night-shift worker that runs your micro-business while you sleep — researching products, drafting listings, replying to customers, and keeping the books. In the morning, you spend 5 minutes as the CEO: approve or reject. Nothing goes live without your decision.

## The problem

I am an international student in Japan. Classes by day, a part-time job at night, and a family back home carrying a NPR 30 lakh home loan. A side business could change everything — but there are no hours left in my day. When I sleep, my business sleeps too.

## How it works

- ☀️ **Watch Mode (day)** — quietly notes new messages and orders, drafts replies, never acts alone.
- 🌙 **Night Shift (while you sleep)** — Muse Spark researches products, drafts listings with prices and margins, prepares customer replies, and updates the books.
- 🌅 **Morning Report (5 min)** — a clear summary of the night's work.
- ✅ **CEO Approval Queue** — approve or reject every action. The owner is always the boss.
- 🧠 **Pattern Learning** — your approvals teach the system your taste, so each night's research gets closer to what you would choose yourself.
- 📊 **Loan Meter** — every approved yen is tracked against the real goal: paying off the family loan.

## Safety by design

- **Demo mode with a hard ¥0 spending block** — no real money is ever spent.
- Human approval is required for anything customer-facing.

## Built with

- Meta Model API — Muse Spark (reasoning, research, drafting)
- React + Vite (web app)
